## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an optimistic, humane vision of the technological future for students and young builders architecting the world of 2050. Rejecting dystopian cliches, sterile wireframes, and aggressive cyberpunk neon-on-black tropes, the aesthetic is bright, breathing, and fundamentally encouraging. 

The visual style blends **Atmospheric Minimalism** with **Tactile Glass & Luminous Accents**. Generous whitespace creates psychological room for contemplation and deep focus. Interfaces are anchored by organic, friendly geometry, ambient diffused teal glows, and subtle technical precision accents—such as micro-grid alignment dots, delicate status rings, and hairline borders—conveying institutional rigor tempered with youthful curiosity.

## Colors

The palette balances clean mineral whites with energizing, luminous teal gradients and deep slate readability.

### Roles & Applications
- **Primary (`#0D9488`)**: Core brand accent, active navigation, primary action button backgrounds, key milestones, and interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6`)**: Luminous highlights, secondary interactive states, progress fill bars, and ambient inner glow overlays.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E`)**: High-contrast interactive anchors, deep teal hover states, and critical data labels on light backgrounds.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep slate for high-contrast headline typography, primary icons, and structural boundaries.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#334155`)**: Slate for body prose, readable descriptions, and supportive messaging.
- **Neutral Muted (`#64748B`)**: Captions, timestamps, disabled indicators, and meta-tokens.
- **Surfaces & Canvas**: Base canvas sits on `#FAFAFA`, with elevated cards and panels on `#FFFFFF`. Secondary container modules utilize subtle mineral fills (`#F4F6F6`).
- **Glow & Accent Tints (`#CCFBF1`, `#F0FDFA`)**: Used strictly for soft contextual halos, highlight pill backgrounds, active badge fills, and delicate glow underlays.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is unified under Plus Jakarta Sans, selected for its crisp geometric construction, high aperture, and friendly humanist undertones.

- **Headlines**: Set tightly with negative letter-spacing to build authority and visual impact. Display scales capture visionary concepts and big milestones.
- **Body Text**: Conversational and spacious. Line heights remain generous (1.5–1.6x) to preserve reading comfort during sustained educational modules.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Formatted with slightly increased tracking and medium-to-bold weights to guarantee legibility on small-form factor cards, status pills, and interactive switches.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to frame expansive learning environments.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column grid, max-width constrained to 1280px or 1440px for wide displays. 48px canvas margin (`margin`), 24px column gutters (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px)**: 8-column grid. 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters. Content stacks into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (Below 640px)**: 4-column grid. 20px canvas margins (`margin-mobile`), 16px gutters (`gutter-mobile`). Full horizontal bleed for learning pathways with horizontal swipe carousels.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical rhythms follow strict multiples of 8px. Spacing between distinct learning units and module chapters relies on large open clearances (`space-xl`), allowing young builders to process dense AI curricula without cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, atmospheric light rather than harsh structural drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Base surface `#FAFAFA` with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting)**: `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a hairline stroke (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Casts an ultra-diffused micro-shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Panels)**: Subtle lift accompanied by a faint teal-tinted ambient glow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Border transitions to `#CCFBF1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: Floated with `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10)`. Backdrops use a blurred glass wash (`background: rgba(250, 250, 250, 0.8)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Technical Glow Accents**: Active progress states and active nodes deploy a luminous aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px 2px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary utilizes modern rounded geometry with pill-shaped ergonomics for interactive components, reinforcing warmth and accessibility.

- **Cards & Content Panels**: Use 16px radius (`rounded-lg`) to balance structural architecture with soft physical boundaries.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Interactive Chips**: Standardized on fully rounded pill silhouettes (`9999px`), inviting intuitive touch and click interaction.
- **Form Inputs & Search**: Formatted with 12px radius (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`) for clean, pocketed text entry.
- **Tech Accent Details**: Secondary micro-indicators (progress dots, status beacons) utilize precise 4px circles and delicate 1px perimeter tracks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid teal (`#0D9488`) fill, white text, pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers a brightness shift to `#14B8A6` and a soft luminous glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.35)`). Active state scales subtly (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure white background, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover triggers `#F0FDFA` background with a `#0D9488` border transition.
- **Luminous Action**: Gradient fill from `#0D9488` to `#14B8A6`, with white typography for milestone achievements or AI model generation triggers.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped inline badges.
- **Active / Learning State**: `#F0FDFA` background, `#0D9488` text, accompanied by an animated pulsing teal status dot (`#14B8A6`) framed with a subtle 1px border.
- **Neutral Meta Chip**: `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, used for tags like "Prompt Engineering", "Ethics", or "2050 Systems".

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 16px rounded corners, and a 1px border in `#F1F5F9`.
- **Interactive Builder Cards**: Include an subtle top inner gradient border highlight. On hover, the card glides upward by 3px with the primary accent glow activating along the bottom edge.

### Input Fields
- White surface, 12px radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, slate placeholder (`#94A3B8`).
- **Focus State**: Hairline transition to `#0D9488` border accompanied by an ambient teal focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(204, 251, 241, 0.65)`). No abrupt browser outline.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes feature a 6px soft-corner box; radios are circular.
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Checked: `#0D9488` fill with crisp white internal icon mark and faint outer teal ring.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Vision Horizon Tracker (Timeline)**: A subtle vertical or horizontal dashed spine (`#CCFBF1`) connecting interactive educational nodes from "Today" through "2050". Active nodes radiate a continuous soft pulse.
- **AI Prompt Workbench Panel**: Modular card incorporating an integrated slate top bar, monospace secondary code views, and a warm-luminous run button.
- **Future Readiness Gauge**: Radial or linear progress tracks filled with a teal-to-luminous-teal gradient, set against neutral `#F1F5F9` troughs.
