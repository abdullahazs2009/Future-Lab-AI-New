Why I Made This

A note from Maya • PYF Core Steward

I started coding when tutorials promised that learning one specific JavaScript framework would guarantee a lifelong career. Ten years later, that framework is history.

Too much current AI discourse is sensationalist noise designed to make students anxious about obsolescence. I created Future Proof AI with the PYF team because durable engineering isn't about chasing the weekly shiny model—it is about mathematical clarity, systems empathy, and the quiet joy of understanding how machines truly think.

Crafted with hope for generation 2050.
