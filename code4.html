Smart question. LLMs mutate quarterly, but rock-solid design principles last decades. Here is your 3-pillar resilience blueprint:

1 Decouple State from the Model

Never let student memories live inside a vendor's contextual window. Store comprehension history as open graph schemas. The model is just a transient engine, not your database!

2 Strict Schema Contracts

Force AI outputs through strict JSON TypeBox/Zod validation layers. When GPT-6 or Claude-5 arrives, your UI pipeline won't shatter from spontaneous tone changes.

3 Multi-Model Fallback Mesh

Route heavy synthetic reasoning to frontier cloud models, and instant flashcard quizzes to lightweight offline WebLLM runtimes on device.
