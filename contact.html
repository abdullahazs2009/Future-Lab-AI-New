Builder Field Guide

Questions people actually ask

Straightforward answers for curious students, teachers, and young builders. Tap a question to open it.

Will current AI programming languages still exist in 2050?

Languages like Python and JavaScript evolve but persist — what changes fastest are the AI models themselves. That’s exactly why we teach you to separate your app’s structure from whichever model powers it today.

How can high school and college students prepare for 2050 AI jobs?

Focus on fundamentals: systems design, data schemas, verification, and ethics. Model providers will keep swapping; builders who understand durable architecture will always be needed.

What if AI surpasses human coders before 2030?

Then the people who specify what should be built — with clear contracts, guardrails, and human-centered goals — become more valuable, not less. Coding gets cheaper; judgment doesn’t.

Is Future Proof AI really free for students?

Yes. The lessons and this website are free forever, supported by the Public Youth Foundation. The only optional cost is your own Gemini API key if you want to use the Lab Chat — and that has a generous free tier.
